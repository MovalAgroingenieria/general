======================
hr_expense_activities
======================

Overview
========
This module extends **Odoo** functionality related to human resources and document management.

It includes the **"Ir Attachment PRL"** feature, which introduces additional document management capabilities for **Occupational Risk Prevention (PRL)**, enabling easier handling and tracking of employee-related certificates, contracts, and safety documentation.

=================
Ir Attachment PRL
=================
* Adds a new main menu called **"PRL"** for managing attachments related to occupational risk prevention.
* Provides additional metadata fields such as document type, expiration date, worker, and contracting company.
* Automatically computes the document state (valid or expired).
* Supports linking attachments to workers or contracting companies automatically when marked as PRL.
