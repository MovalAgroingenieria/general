Management of primary entities and secondary members with configurable relationships.

Allows marking ``res.partner`` records as *primary entities* (organizations) or
*secondary members* (individuals/groups), and managing their relationships with
local codes, sequences, and optional global codes.

Configuration
=============

Go to **Settings > Users & Companies > Users** and assign the appropriate role:

* **General Entity / User**: can view all entity and member data (read-only).
* **General Entity / Manager**: full create, edit, and delete access.
  Administrators are assigned this role automatically.
