Adds period census management (monthly snapshots) to primary entities.

Each census captures the list of active members with their quantities at a given
point in time. Censuses can be locked to prevent further editing, and the lines
from the previous period can be copied to speed up data entry.

Configuration
=============

Roles are inherited from the *Base General Entity* module:

* **General Entity / User**: can view censuses and census lines (read-only).
* **General Entity / Manager**: can create, edit, lock/unlock, and delete censuses.
